🏥 MediNova AI

AI-Powered Smart Healthcare Assistant

React • TypeScript • Vite • Gemini AI • Express • Tailwind CSS

🌍 About the Project

MediNova AI is an AI-powered healthcare web application designed to help users quickly access reliable health information, understand common symptoms, receive medicine-related guidance, and improve their overall health awareness through Artificial Intelligence.

Instead of searching multiple websites or reading confusing medical articles, users can ask health-related questions in natural language and instantly receive structured, easy-to-understand responses generated by Google Gemini AI.

The application combines modern web technologies with Artificial Intelligence to provide an accessible, responsive, and user-friendly healthcare experience for students, families, and anyone seeking general medical guidance.

Mission: To make basic healthcare information easily accessible through AI while encouraging responsible medical decision-making and consultation with healthcare professionals.

⚠️ Disclaimer

MediNova AI provides general healthcare information and educational guidance only. It is not a replacement for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you experience severe symptoms or a medical emergency, immediately contact your local emergency services or consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Many people struggle to obtain trustworthy healthcare information quickly. Searching multiple websites often leads to confusion, misinformation, and unnecessary anxiety.

Common challenges include:

Difficulty understanding medical terminology.
Uncertainty about common symptoms.
Lack of quick access to reliable health guidance.
Information scattered across different websites.
💡 Solution

MediNova AI solves these problems by providing an intelligent AI healthcare assistant capable of:

Providing general symptom guidance.
Offering medicine-related educational information.
Promoting healthy lifestyle recommendations.
Encouraging users to seek professional medical care whenever necessary.

The application delivers fast, AI-generated responses through a clean and responsive interface.
